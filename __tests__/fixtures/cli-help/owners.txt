pmcp owners
  Who looks after a path, from [[owners]] in pmcp.toml

  pmcp owners <path>... [--config <file>] [--json]

  Options
    --config <file>  A pmcp.toml to read instead of the nearest one.
    --json           Print the result as one JSON envelope on stdout.

  Examples
    pmcp owners services/auth/src/login.ts
    pmcp owners docs --json
